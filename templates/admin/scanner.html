Local Network Subnet Configuration Guide

To test live automatic Wi-Fi detection on your local network:

1. Find Your Router / LAN Subnet:
  • Windows: Open terminal, run ipconfig. Look for your IPv4 Address (e.g. 192.168.29.24). Your subnet is 192.168.29.0/24.
  • Linux/macOS: Run ip route | grep default or route -n get default.
2. Register Your Phone or Laptop:
  • Go to the Register Device portal.
  • Find your phone's real Wi-Fi MAC address and enter it.
  • Ensure your phone is connected to the same Wi-Fi router.
  • Click "Scan Network Now" above and watch the attendance record get added automatically!
